# Role and problem statement

**Role: Forward-deployed Engineering** (second choice: Technical Product Management).

Iya Bisi runs a home kitchen in Surulere and sells lunch and dinner to offices and homes nearby over WhatsApp. She answers the same three questions all day, takes orders by hand, matches bank transfers to names from screenshots, and oversells dishes because the portion count lives in her head. The agent shows today's menu from her admin panel, takes the order, collects payment through a Paystack link, and records the paid order with stock reduced, so she cooks instead of typing and never confirms an order that was not paid. It deliberately leaves out delivery dispatch, refunds, multiple vendors and building the menu from photos.
