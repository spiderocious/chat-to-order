# Role and problem statement

**Role: Forward-deployed Engineering** (second choice: Technical Product Management).

TPlus Kitchen is a real food and small-chops vendor in Osogbo that takes every order on WhatsApp (https://www.instagram.com/teeplus_events/). The owner answers the same three questions all day, takes orders by hand, matches bank transfers to names from screenshots, and oversells dishes because the portion count lives in one person's head. The agent shows today's menu from the admin panel, takes the order, collects payment through a Paystack link, and records the paid order with stock reduced, so the kitchen cooks instead of typing and never confirms an order that was not paid. It deliberately leaves out delivery dispatch, refunds, multiple vendors and building the menu from photos.
